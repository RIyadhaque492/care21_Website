Urgent help

If your child is in danger, call 999 now. 999 is Bangladesh’s national emergency number.

Get help right away if you see

  • • Trouble breathing, or blue lips
  • • Your child will not feed, or keeps vomiting
  • • A seizure
  • • Neck pain, new weakness, trouble walking, or loss of bladder control
  • • A fever that does not settle
Call 999
